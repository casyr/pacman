Controls Movement

press w to move up
press d to move right
press a to move left
press s to move down

Game Rules

The Objective is to navigate the maze
and eat all the pac-gums on the board
to achieve victory.

Watch out for the ghosts your enemies
patrolling the corridors! Each has its
own AI and pursuit behavior.

If they touch you, you lose the game.
Observe their movement.

Superpacgum bigger dot can provide you
the power to eat ghosts and collect
more points.

Cheat mode is implemented if you want.



Press 1 to return main menu
